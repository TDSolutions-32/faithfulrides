Privacy policy

How this website handles your information. Last updated October 22, 2026.

What we collect

When you send a ride request or message through this website, we receive the details you enter, such as your name, phone number, addresses and trip details. Forms are delivered to us by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service, which emails your submission to contact@faithfulridesllc.com.

If you call, email or send us a message, we use what you share only to respond to you and to arrange transportation.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If you change the text size, your browser remembers that choice on your own device. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Protecting your information

We use appropriate practices to protect the information you share with us against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. Please don’t send medical records by email.

Other websites

Our pages link to other websites, such as Medicare.gov, Onslow County and Google Maps. We don’t control those sites and aren’t responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we’ll change the date at the top of this page.
