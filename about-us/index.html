About Faithful Rides

Safe, reliable transportation you can count on — from a veteran-owned, family-oriented team that cares about the community it serves.

Driven by care, guided by faith

At Faithful Rides, we believe transportation is more than getting from one place to another. It’s about connection, care and commitment.

We specialize in non-emergency medical transportation that puts your comfort, safety and schedule first. Whether you need a ride to a routine appointment, a therapy session or a hospital discharge, our professional drivers ensure timely, courteous service — every time.

We serve individuals, healthcare providers and caregivers with the dependable support they deserve: compassionate, punctual, flexible, committed and safe.

Why choose Faithful Rides

  • Veteran owned and operated

    A veteran-owned, family-oriented company that cares about the people and community we serve.

  • Prompt and reliable

    We arrive on time and make sure you get home safely.

  • Friendly, professional drivers

    Every driver is screened and held to our high standards of courtesy and care.

  • Most insurances accepted

    We accept most major insurances to keep your out-of-pocket costs down. Private pay is welcome too.

  • Fair, competitive rates

    We keep our prices competitive so every ride is a good value.

  • 100% satisfaction guarantee

    If you are ever unhappy with a ride, we will do everything we can to make it right.
