How to schedule a ride

  1. Call or request a ride

    Call (252) 503-2978 or send a ride request online with your questions.

  2. Share the details

    Tell us the appointment date, time and location, plus where to pick you up.

  3. We confirm your ride

    We call to confirm and make sure everything is ready for your trip.

  4. We arrive and help

    Your driver arrives on time and assists you throughout the journey — then home again.

Common questions

Do you help passengers who use a wheelchair?

Yes. Our vehicles are equipped to help passengers who use walkers, canes or wheelchairs, and our drivers are trained in passenger assistance.

Can I set up rides on a regular schedule?

Yes. We offer recurring ride scheduling for ongoing treatments like dialysis or therapy.

Do you take insurance?

We accept most major insurances, and private pay is welcome. Call (252) 503-2978 and we’ll help you understand your options. Our NPI for insurance use is 1760299101.

Is this an ambulance service?

No. We provide non-emergency transportation for people who need help getting to care but don’t need an ambulance. In an emergency, call 911.

Can a family member book a ride for a parent?

Absolutely. Many of our rides are scheduled by caregivers, family members and healthcare providers.

Need a ride to an appointment?

Call us or send a ride request. We’ll confirm your trip and take care of the rest.
