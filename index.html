More than a ride to the curb

From the moment we knock to the moment you’re settled back home, you’re treated with patience, dignity and understanding.

  • Trained, compassionate drivers

    Trained in passenger assistance and safety, and patient with anyone who needs extra time or care.

  • Accessible vehicles

    Our vehicles are equipped to help passengers who use walkers, canes or wheelchairs.

  • Door-to-door support

    We don’t just pull up to the curb. We help you from your home, into the vehicle and into your destination.

  • Recurring rides

    Standing schedules for ongoing treatments like dialysis or therapy, so you don’t have to call every time.

Booking is simple

Most rides are set up with one phone call.

  1. Call or request a ride

    Call (252) 503-2978 or send a ride request online with your questions.

  2. Share the details

    Tell us the appointment date, time and location, plus where to pick you up.

  3. We confirm your ride

    We call to confirm and make sure everything is ready for your trip.

  4. We arrive and help

    Your driver arrives on time and assists you throughout the journey — then home again.
